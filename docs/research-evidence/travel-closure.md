# FAA closure scope and window, 25 November 2026

The recorded FAA fixture and a fresh feed both list LAX, SAN and LAS as closed specifically to non-scheduled transient general aviation, not all airport traffic. Those exact restrictions are now excluded from airport-wide disruption and retained per airport with full original reason and explicit exclusion. PPR alone, helicopter exceptions and other general closures remain disruptions. Ground stops, GDP and arrival/departure delays are unchanged. Response text discloses excluded notices; receipt retains original feed and exclusions. Status remains airport-level only, never flight status.

Closure display Start/Reopen dates have no year. Rather than silently assign one, parse the explicit YYMMDDHHmm-YYMMDDHHmm UTC range in the NOTAM reason, validate calendar fields and start<end, and evaluate start-inclusive/end-exclusive against feed Update_Time. Missing, permanent, malformed or unsupported ranges refuse the requested airport verdict with FAA_CLOSURE_WINDOW_UNRESOLVED, never become undisrupted. This narrow parser does not claim all NOTAM syntax. It deliberately does not infer a year from the current month or strip every PPR closure.

Source https://nasstatus.faa.gov/api/airport-status-information ; FAA user guide https://nasstatus.faa.gov/static/media/NASStatusUserGuide.cccc6d48.pdf notes closure exceptions and UTC event dates.
