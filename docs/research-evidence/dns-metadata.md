# DNS response metadata, 25 November 2026

The scored has_record policy is unchanged: conclusive resolver existence agreement only, refusing existence disagreement or total failure. Responses now include each answering resolver's records, matching-record minimum TTL and AD assertion, plus the serving resolver's alias chain and TTL. TTL is null when absent, malformed or no requested records exist; it is seconds, not an observation timestamp. AD is a resolver assertion, not independent DNSSEC verification. No aggregate DNSSEC claim is made. record_values_agree compares exact data-string sets across at least two answering resolvers, ignoring order/duplicates; one resolver yields null. It does not establish global agreement or semantic equivalence of differently encoded data.

Production parsing binds matching records to the requested owner, following an unambiguous CNAME chain for non-CNAME queries. Unrelated same-type answer owners, cycles and conflicting alias targets refuse that resolver. CNAME queries return aliases owned by the requested name. No new source, polling, account or registration.
